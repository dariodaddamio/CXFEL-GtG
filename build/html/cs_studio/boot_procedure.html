Standard Boot Procedure¶

  1. Connect to FortiClient, and RealVNC.

  2. Open a terminal on your machine.

  3. Start the GUI (Phoebus / CS-Studio):

    • Run: phoebus.sh

  4. If you see extra/unwanted tabs, close them. We are looking for a tab that says “CS-Studio: CXFEL Home” or just “CS-Studio”.

If the page you are left with is blank and has no label after “CS-Studio:”, click the blue home icon to reach the desired screen.

  • Once you have reached the home screen, you can start to navigate the GUI.
